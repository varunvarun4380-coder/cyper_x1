About cyperX & AIT

Adithya Institute of Technology

Adithya Institute of Technology (AIT) was established in 2008 by the Adithya Education Trust with the noble mission of imparting value-based education. Located on the Sathy Main Road in Kurumbapalayam, Coimbatore, AIT is approved by AICTE, New Delhi, and is affiliated to Anna University, Chennai.

AIT has emerged as a premier "Industry Integrated Institution" that bridges corporate needs with academic training. We are recognized as an Autonomous Institution and accredited by NAAC with a prestigious **A+ Grade**, demonstrating our commitment to excellence in technical education, infrastructure, and student placement.

cyperX Symposium

cyperX is the annual National Level Technical Symposium hosted by the engineering departments of Adithya Institute of Technology. This event provides a platform for students from civil, computer science, electronics, electrical, mechanical, and informational disciplines to showcase their technical prowess, innovative concepts, and creative talents.

With events ranging from technical paper presentations to thrilling roboteering races, coding challenges, and non-technical games, cyperX promises to be an event of outstanding educational exchange and competitive thrill.

Rules & Guidelines

  • All student participants must carry their valid College Identity Cards. Without an ID card, entry to the campus will be restricted.
  • Participants are requested to follow a formal or decent smart-casual dress code fitting a professional college symposium.
  • For technical presentations, maximum time allowed is 7 minutes for presentation and 3 minutes for Q&A. Maximum 3 members per team.
  • The registration fee is Rs. 150/- per participant. Each participant can enter multiple events (subject to scheduling constraints).
  • Judges' decisions will be final and binding for all competitions. Disrespectful behavior may lead to immediate disqualification.
